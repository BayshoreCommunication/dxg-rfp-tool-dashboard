Event Overview

The Global Innovation Summit 2026 is Apex Dynamics' flagship annual conference, bringing together more than 1,000 senior executives, technology leaders, and industry innovators for three days of keynotes, interactive sessions, and high-level networking.

This year's event marks the debut of a full hybrid format, extending the Summit's reach to a global virtual audience for the first time. The production must deliver an immersive, broadcast-quality experience both in the room and on screen — with a cinematic 120-foot curved LED wall as the visual centerpiece of the general session stage.

Apex Dynamics is seeking an AV and production partner who can match the ambition of this event: technically flawless, creatively sharp, and operationally dependable from load-in through strike.
